1. About this app

If We Never Said Goodbye (package name: com.mmlucky.ifweneversaidgoodbye) is an Android fiction-reading and text-to-speech audiobook application. It offers novels in seven languages: English, Chinese, Malay, Thai, Indonesian, Tamil, and Filipino.

The app does not require you to create an account or submit your name, email address, or telephone number to read the stories.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide reading and listening features, the app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Chosen language, last-read chapter, reading progress, and visited chapters.
  • Bookmarks, font-size preferences, and display theme.
  • Text-to-speech settings, such as speech rate, voice selection, automatic next chapter, and text-following preferences.
  • A count of manually opened chapters, used to determine when an interstitial advertisement may be shown.

The app does not operate its own user-account database or upload these reading preferences to an app-controlled server. Android's device backup settings may back up or restore some local app data.

3. Advertising and third-party data processing

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner and interstitial ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and approximate location inferred from IP address, as permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.

Google and its partners may use this information to provide, personalize where permitted, measure, and protect ads, including fraud prevention. This advertising-related processing is performed by third parties under their own policies.

For more information, see Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies information.

4. Advertising consent and choices

When required, the app uses Google's consent management tools to request your advertising choices. If available for your region and configuration, you can review your choices in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options. You may also manage certain advertising settings through your Android device or Google account. Availability of consent controls and ad types varies by jurisdiction and device.

5. Text-to-speech audiobook feature

The listening feature uses a speech engine available through Android or the browser environment. Depending on the installed engine and voice, chapter text may be processed on your device or sent to that speech engine's service for synthesis. The app does not request microphone access, record your voice, or independently upload voice recordings. Third-party speech engines are subject to their own privacy practices.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Local reading and audiobook settings generally remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Some information may be restored from an Android device backup, depending on your settings. You can clear app data through Android Settings. Information processed by Google or speech providers is governed by their respective retention and deletion policies.

7. Children and age suitability

The app is a romance-fiction experience and is not specifically designed for children. It contains fictional themes involving relationships and emotional conflict. Parents and guardians should use the Google Play content rating and their own judgment when assessing suitability.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if app features, advertising practices, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

1. 關於本應用程式

If We Never Said Goodbye（套件名稱：com.mmlucky.ifweneversaidgoodbye）是一款 Android 小說閱讀與文字轉語音朗讀 App，提供英文、中文、馬來文、泰文、印尼文、坦米爾文及菲律賓文七種語言內容。

閱讀小說不需要註冊帳號，也不需要提供姓名、電子郵件或電話號碼。

2. 儲存在裝置上的資料

為了提供閱讀及朗讀功能，App 會在你的裝置本機儲存：

  • 語言選擇、上次閱讀的章節、閱讀進度及曾開啟的章節。
  • 書籤、字體大小與畫面主題設定。
  • 朗讀速度、語音選擇、自動下一章與文字跟隨等設定。
  • 手動開啟章節的次數，用於判斷何時嘗試顯示插頁廣告。

本 App 不設置自己的使用者帳號資料庫，也不會把上述閱讀偏好上傳到 App 自有伺服器。部分本機資料可能依照 Android 裝置備份設定進行備份或還原。

3. 廣告與第三方資料處理

本 App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示 Banner 橫幅及插頁廣告。Google Mobile Ads SDK 及其廣告合作夥伴可能依適用法律與你的同意選擇，自動收集或處理廣告識別碼、IP 位址、裝置及 App 資訊、廣告互動、診斷資訊，以及由 IP 推估的大致位置。

第三方可能使用相關資訊提供廣告、在允許的情況下進行個人化、衡量成效及防止詐欺。這些活動依各第三方自己的隱私政策進行。

詳情請參閱 Google 隱私權政策及Google 廣告技術說明。

4. 廣告同意與選擇

在適用情況下，App 會使用 Google 的同意管理工具取得廣告相關選擇。如所在地區及 App 設定支援，可在 Settings → Ad privacy options 查看相關選項，也可以透過 Android 裝置或 Google 帳號管理部分廣告設定。實際選項因地區和裝置而異。

5. 文字轉語音朗讀

朗讀功能使用 Android 或瀏覽器環境提供的語音引擎。依照所安裝的語音引擎及語音版本，章節文字可能在裝置內處理，或傳送至語音引擎服務合成語音。本 App 不要求麥克風權限、不錄製你的聲音，也不會自行上傳語音錄音。第三方語音引擎適用其各自的隱私規定。

6. 資料保存與刪除

本機閱讀及朗讀設定通常會保留至你清除 App 儲存資料或解除安裝為止；部分資料可能依 Android 備份設定還原。你可透過 Android 設定清除 App 資料。Google 與語音服務供應商的資料保存及刪除方式以其各自政策為準。

7. 兒童及內容適齡性

本 App 以虛構愛情小說為主，並非專門為兒童設計。內容涉及人際關係、感情及情緒衝突。家長或監護人可參考 Google Play 內容分級判斷是否適合閱讀。
